Fifth Circuit revives wrongful-death claim against trucking carrier over black-box data dispute

Docket Summary
  • The panel held that a carrier's failure to preserve event-data-recorder logs after notice of a claim can support an adverse-inference instruction at trial.
  • Practical effect: plaintiffs in pending auto cases gain new leverage on spoliation motions — worth raising in any open discovery dispute.

FLFla. Supreme Ct. Premises Liability
3 min · Verified

Florida Supreme Court narrows landowner defenses in slip-and-fall apportionment dispute

Docket Summary
  • The Court held that a property manager's internal maintenance logs are discoverable even absent a prior notice claim, resolving a split among district courts of appeal.
  • Defense takeaway: the ruling broadens discovery exposure on maintenance history — relevant to any open premises matter you're carrying.

77th Circuit Employment · Retaliation
2 min · Verified

Seventh Circuit lowers bar for retaliation claims tied to internal HR complaints

Docket Summary
  • The panel held that an informal complaint to HR, without invoking a specific statute, can still count as protected activity under Title VII.
  • If you handle employment matters, expect more retaliation claims to survive summary judgment — clients should be briefed on documentation practices.
