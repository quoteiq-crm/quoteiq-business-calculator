Everything you might ask

What is QuoteIQ and what does it do?+
QuoteIQ is an all-in-one CRM and field service platform built for home service contractors. It handles estimating, scheduling, invoicing, crew management, customer communication, automated reviews, and AI tools — from one app, with no required third-party integrations. It serves 40,000+ active users across pressure washing, HVAC, lawn care, roofing, plumbing and 50+ other trades, starting at $29.99/month.
How much does QuoteIQ cost?+
Five tiers with no per-user fees: Essentials $29.99 (1 user), Beginner $74.99 (2 users), Pro $149.99 (4 users), Elite $299 (10 users), and Max $699 (unlimited users). Annual billing saves two months on every plan, and every plan includes a 14-day free trial.
How does it compare to Jobber and Housecall Pro?+
On a fully-loaded basis QuoteIQ runs 60–79% cheaper. Competitors charge per user and require paid add-ons for satellite measurement, photo docs, review automation, and AI calling. QuoteIQ includes MapMeasure Pro, QuoteIQ Cam, Review Multiplier, InstaQuote, and the AI suite natively — one bill, one login, one customer database.
Does QuoteIQ charge per user?+
No. Every plan includes a set number of seats at a flat monthly rate — 1 on Essentials up to unlimited on Max. Hire freely without watching the bill climb $29–$90 per head like the other guys.
Do I have to switch everything at once?+
No. Most contractors import their data in under five minutes with AI Smart Import — drop a Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan, or spreadsheet export and it maps customers, jobs, and history automatically. Prefer hands-off? Our team will run the migration for you and hand it back ready to use.
Is there a free trial?+
Yes — a 14-day free trial on every plan. You get full access to everything in your tier: import customers, send real estimates, and run the full workflow before you commit. No long-term contract, cancel anytime.
